What to fix first

Findings

Each finding is a shape of excessive rights, attached to the object that would fix it. A privileged account signed in on an ordinary workstation is the classic one: whoever administers that workstation can take the credential.

Shortest path

From one object to another, one step at a time: an endpoint exposes the accounts signed in on it, an account is a member of its groups, and an account or group administers the endpoints it holds a grant on. The path is what an attacker would actually do.
