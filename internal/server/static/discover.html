Everything on the network

Assembled from endpoints' own ARP and neighbour tables — no sweep, no packets on the wire, nothing an IDS will flag. Anything that talks to anything shows up in someone's table, so this finds devices a scan misses: a printer or camera that ignores ping still appears in the table of whatever it last spoke to. Devices are matched by MAC, since a device that changes address is the same device.

Names come from reverse DNS: point Mustra at the DNS server that hands out DHCP leases (usually your router) below. A device seen for the first time raises an alert, except devices Mustra manages and randomized (private) MACs, which phones rotate. Label a device to remember what it is; ignore it to stop counting it.
