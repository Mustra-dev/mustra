Vulnerability levels

One level per endpoint, the way Comply states it: the worst actionable severity if the endpoint was scanned in the last 30 days; no vulnerabilities for a clean fresh scan; not scored when findings have no published severity; not scanned for anything older, never scanned, or on a platform the scanner does not cover. A level from a stale scan is a guess wearing a label, so it is not stated.
