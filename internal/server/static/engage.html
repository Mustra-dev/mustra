Surveys

A survey rides the same channel as a notification — the Self Service window, with a toast as the nudge — and stores a different kind of answer. The sentiment score is the average of the scoreable answers, nothing cleverer: ratings map 1–5 onto 0–100, and a choice counts only where you have said which options are the good ones. Free text is never scored, because inferring a mood from prose by counting words produces a number nobody can check.

Under 5 answers no score is given. A low response rate is said out loud rather than hidden behind the number, because the people who answer an unprompted survey are rarely the indifferent ones. “No thanks” is recorded as its own answer — it is the only thing separating a question nobody wanted from one nobody was asked — and it is counted, never scored.
