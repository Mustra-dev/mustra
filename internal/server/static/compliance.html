Hardening score per endpoint

Each endpoint is checked against the benchmark written for its operating system and version (the CIS policies under Policies; the nearest version when there is no exact one, marked ≈). The score is the share of evaluated checks that pass, weighted by severity (critical 4, high 3, medium 2, low 1); checks that could not be evaluated and checks under an exception are left out. The line is the last 30 days. Download a report as CSV for one endpoint or the whole fleet.

Controls

Export CSV

Each control lists the benchmark checks that give evidence for it, as the benchmark itself maps them; mustra adds no mapping of its own to imported benchmarks, so a control with no mapped check is not shown rather than shown as met. A control fails when any of its checks fails on any endpoint, and a check that could not be evaluated keeps its control unknown, never passing. Exceptions count for neither. This is evidence for an assessment, not an assessment: most controls also need policy and process no endpoint scan can show.

Exceptions

A check you have decided not to apply — because it conflicts with how this site works, or needs a reinstall — stops counting against the hardening score. It still runs and its real result is kept; it shows as excepted. An exception matches the check by its title, so it covers every benchmark version that has it, and applies to every endpoint or to one group. Give the reason: an exception nobody can explain later is just a hidden failure.
