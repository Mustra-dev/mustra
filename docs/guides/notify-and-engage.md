# Notify and Engage
<!-- group: Communicate; order: 50; pages: /eun, /engage -->

Talk to the people using your endpoints: show them a message or a restart prompt (Notify), or ask them how things are going (Engage).

## Before you start

- Sending messages and surveys needs **Deploy software and run actions** (`deploy.manage`). Reading results needs **See the fleet**.
- The person must be signed in to the endpoint to see anything. Messages appear in a window from the mustra agent's tray helper.

## Send a message (Notify)

1. Open **Notify → Messages** and fill in **New message**: a title and the text.
2. For a restart prompt, set the **Restart options**: whether people may postpone, and for how long.
3. Choose **Add a button** for each choice you offer (for example "Restart now", "Remind me in an hour"). **Preview** shows exactly what they will see.
4. Choose **Save message**.
5. Under **Send a message**, choose the message and the endpoints or groups, and **Send now**.

Each person's answer is recorded and shown against the message. A playbook can also send a message as one of its steps.

## Ask a question (Engage)

1. Open **Engage → Surveys** and choose **New survey**.
2. Choose **Add a question** for each question: a rating, a choice or free text.
3. Choose **Save survey**.
4. Under **Ask the fleet**, choose the survey and the endpoints or groups, and **Ask**.
5. Open **Results** on the survey. Scores appear once at least five people have answered, so no one person's answer can be singled out.
